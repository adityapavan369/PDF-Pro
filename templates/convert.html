Converter engines explained

advanced: Uses repository advanced converter which tries multiple strategies to preserve layout and reduce page count (best available in-app).

soffice: LibreOffice headless — high fidelity conversion preserving layout, fonts, images.

docx2pdf: Uses MS Word (Windows/macOS) via docx2pdf library — high fidelity when Word is installed.

pillow: Image-to-PDF conversion (images only).

fallback: Minimal python-docx → ReportLab conversion — text only, low fidelity.
